Uncertainty in vulnerability of networks under attack

Alireza Ermagun, Nazanin Tajik, and Hani Mahmassani

Scientific Reports · 13, 3179 (2023)

Article ↗BibTeX ↓
Research summary

This study examines how network structure and size shape performance loss under disruption. It distinguishes the extent of vulnerability from uncertainty in that vulnerability, providing a framework for thinking about preparedness and recovery across different network configurations.

Multiple-Drones-Multiple-Trucks Routing Problem for Disruption Assessment

Alireza Ermagun and Nazanin Tajik

Transportation Research Record · 2677(2), 725-740 (2023)

Article ↗BibTeX ↓
Research summary

This study develops a coordinated drone-and-truck routing approach for assessing disrupted infrastructure. A mixed-integer optimization model and a greedy heuristic support resource deployment and scheduling, with experiments on problems drawn from the Minneapolis-St. Paul freeway system.
